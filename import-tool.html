How to use
1

Paste your symbols

Enter ticker symbols in the Input box — one per line, or comma-separated. No exchange prefix needed.

2

Pick exchange & convert

Select NSE, BSE, or MCX above, then hit Convert. The output is formatted as EXCHANGE:SYMBOL.

3

Copy & import

Click Copy to clipboard, then paste into Watchlist Pro's Import dialog. Up to 1000 symbols at once.
